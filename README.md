## 💕 About Resin Bloom

*Resin Bloom* is a simple and attractive handmade resin art website created to showcase beautiful customized resin products.

The website allows customers to explore resin creations, select their favorite product, and submit their customization and delivery details through an order form.

---

## 🌸 Our Collection

### 🌿 Floral Coaster
Beautiful botanical resin coasters designed for home decoration.

## 📝 Order Form

Customers can provide:

- 👤 Customer Name
- 🔑 Selected Product
- 📍 Delivery Address
- 🎨 Preferred Colors / Flowers
- ✨ Additional Customization Details

The submitted order information is prepared as an email request.
